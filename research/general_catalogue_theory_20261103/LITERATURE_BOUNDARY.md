# Attribution and the bounded novelty assessment

Checked 2026-11-03. Public queries used generic bibliographic/topic descriptions;
unpublished manuscripts and theory notes were not uploaded. This is not a
systematic review or a literature-wide priority determination.

The earlier [literature note](../top1_decision_theory_20261103/LITERATURE_AND_NOVELTY.md)
remains applicable. Three relevant primary sources were revisited or added:

| Primary source | Established content retained | Boundary for the present result |
|---|---|---|
| [Kulkarni and Tal, On Fractional Block Sensitivity, ECCC revision 1](https://eccc.weizmann.ac.il/report/2013/168/revision/1/download) | Minimal sensitive blocks and fractional certificates, especially Definition 2.1 | The flip-cover LP, its dual and certificate interpretation are prior tools. We claim no new certificate-complexity definition. |
| [Degenne, Koolen and Menard, Non-Asymptotic Pure Exploration by Solving Games, NeurIPS 2019](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2019/hash/8d1de7457fa769ece8d93a13a59c8552-Abstract.html) | General information allocation for structured pure exploration | Replacing state recovery by output identification and formulating the information optimization are established machinery. |
| [Gronlund et al., Fast Exact k-Means, k-Medians and Bregman Divergence Clustering in 1D, arXiv:1701.07204](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.07204) | Ordered scalar clustering for Bregman divergences; v2 section 4.1 discusses linear separators | Sorted groups and two rate pools have familiar one-dimensional clustering structure. The prefix-cut device is not claimed as a new general clustering algorithm. |

Reading scope: the ECCC definition and surrounding material, the NeurIPS
abstract, and the arXiv bibliographic record plus v2 HTML section 4.1 were
available. The first attempt to fetch the clustering author's PDF timed out;
an alternative author's interval-clustering PDF returned 403. The arXiv
version supplied a primary-source fallback. No full-paper reading or successful
access to either failed URL is claimed.

Relative to our previous note, the candidate additions are the exact entropy
equality test with candidate rates eliminated, the unsaturated oracle-face
dimension restriction and unique-optimum corollary, and explicit finite-affine
catalogue constructions showing both rate dependence and insufficiency of
fractional-cover summaries. These are results in our stated channel model;
whether equivalent results appear elsewhere remains unverified. The affine
antichain realization is a construction supporting scope, not a standalone
priority claim.

No claim is made that generic threshold decisions, partial preference
identification, active recommendation, noisy elicitation or convex geometry
are newly introduced here. No real-user validity follows from these sources
or from the controlled constructions.
