\paragraph{A robust necessity of mixed reading depths.}
Consider one fact, all format-composition blocks of depths $1,\ldots,6$,
and extraction parameters bounded above by $39/40$.
Each source carries a persistent report, correct with known probability $r$;
the extraction rates $p>q\ge1/2$ are unknown.
An oracle knows the better format, and receives two labelled calibration
streams, each of length $M_\delta\sim\alpha\log(1/\delta)$.
Let $K_{\rm or}$ denote its optimal asymptotic online-cost coefficient,
and $K_L$ the coefficient restricted to depth $L$, allowing every format
composition at that depth.

\paragraph{Proposition (nondegenerate depth-mixing region).}
Put $\varepsilon=1/40000$. For every
\[
\begin{gathered}
|r-3/5|,\ |q-3/5|,\ |p-19/20|\le\varepsilon,\\
\alpha\in[19/20,6/5],\qquad c/\ell\in[1/2,3/2],
\end{gathered}
\]
where $c$ is the source-access cost and $\ell>0$ the per-read cost,
\[
K_{\rm or}\le16c+18\ell,\qquad
\min_L K_L\ge16c+18\ell+\frac{15}{7}\ell.
\]
Consequently, every optimal allocation uses at least two distinct depths,
with a relative online-cost reduction of at least $5/103$ against every
fixed depth. At the central rates, the same $(\alpha,c/\ell)$ rectangle
admits the stronger additive gap $9\ell/4$ and relative reduction $3/59$.

\paragraph{Proof outline (computer-assisted).}
Normalize $\ell=1$ and use $14$ depth-one and $2$ depth-two good-format
blocks per $\log(1/\delta)$ units. Profiling the alternative low rate
sets $v=\min(q,u)$. A rational interval certificate covering the entire
range $u\in[1/2,39/40]$ gives an information surplus exceeding $1/35$
at the central rates and $\alpha=1$.
The profiled calibration divergence is at most $103/200$ there.
Reducing $\alpha$ to $19/20$ leaves surplus $79/28000$.
Analytic derivatives bound the total sensitivity to $(r,p,q)$ by $100$,
leaving surplus at least $9/28000$ throughout the displayed box.

For depth one, the fair-rate alternative gives
$K_1\ge50(c+1)(1-5151\alpha/10000)$.
For $L=2,\ldots,6$, the same-rate opposite-truth alternative has zero
calibration divergence. A certificate for all format compositions,
including analytic perturbation bounds, gives
$K_L\ge(206/5)(c+L)/(L+1)$.
Subtracting $16c+18$ yields a minimum gap $15/7$ on the rectangle.
Since $16c+18\le42$, the relative saving is at least
$(15/7)/(42+15/7)=5/103$.
The finite-action program attains its optimum, so the strict gap excludes
every allocation supported on one depth. Rescaling costs restores $\ell$.

\paragraph{Scope.}
This is a sufficient parameter region and a pointwise asymptotic result.
It neither establishes a maximal phase boundary nor proves that an optimum
uses only the good format or exactly two depths. Calibration costs are
excluded from the online objective. Real Web validity requires separate evidence.
