# Revised argument and evidence contract

2026-11-05; author-review draft, not submitted.

## Story

An online shopping assistant must learn which preferences could change its
recommendation and how to obtain reliable answers about them. The catalogue
and reliability-sharing structure jointly determine the best achievable
interaction cost. A practical question selector also needs a short-horizon
objective, and this introduces a distinct problem: learning how to ask may
have no immediate recommendation value. We prove a structural boundary,
instantiate a joint-belief decision-value loop, and test the connection rather
than assuming the loop inherits the boundary's efficiency.

## Two contributions, with unequal novelty strength

1. **Structural theory:** exact entropy-cut criterion for zero first-order
   format-learning premium, unique unsaturated allocation corollary and
   equal-group-size catalogue counterexample. Generic characteristic-time
   machinery and fractional certificate quantities are prior work.
2. **Operational bridge and limitation analysis:** joint preference/type/rate
   DV with separate GLR certification; exact model-specific two-step
   complementarity example with a nondegenerate parameter neighborhood;
   frozen paired comparison and a separate real-product disclosure adapter.
   The VOI formula, generic complementarity and safety construction are not
   claimed as independently novel principles. Current data do not support a
   strong new-method-superiority contribution.

## Separation of questions

- Original full-profile recovery and this best-item theorem have different
  alternative sets. Earlier Web-provenance and training work remain separate.
- Optimal information coefficient, practical acquisition score and certificate
  are three different objects. No proof transfers efficiency by substituting DV.
- The example proves possible format-learning complementarity. The 264 paths
  show DV plateau and cost disadvantage; they do not isolate that mechanism
  causally.
- Fashion acceptable-set success permits several purchases and NO_BUY. This
  is not the theorem's unique-winner target. ABSTAIN denotes lack of certificate.
- Binary samples and structured field disclosures are not equal interaction units.
- Historical Luna and Python outputs reuse profiles/menus; do not pool them as
  independent users or report only a flattering aggregate.
